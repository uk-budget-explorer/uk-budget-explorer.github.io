How to interpret these figures

UK public sector net debt excluding public sector banks is a stock measured at the end of a reporting month. The figure displayed above uses the ONS September 2026 provisional estimate. This debt stock is distinct from annual borrowing and from total government expenditure.

The OBR 2026/27 forecast for central government debt interest net of the Asset Purchase Facility is spread evenly over 365 days to illustrate an average rate. Actual interest flows vary, and changes in the debt stock involve many factors beyond interest. Do not add the illustration to published debt to claim a real-time total.

National debt — common questions

Is the national debt number live?

No. It is the published ONS debt stock for the stated month, not a second-by-second observation.

Why does the interest counter move?

It illustrates how a fixed annual OBR forecast translates into an average per second. It is not a tracker of actual payments.

How is debt different from borrowing?

Debt is a stock recorded at a point in time. Borrowing is a flow over a period and is not the same thing as debt interest.
